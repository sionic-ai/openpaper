# Changelog

The app shows the section for its version once, after an update. Keep each entry short and about what users notice.

## 0.2.23

- Review preprints read cleanly: margin line numbers are gone, two columns read in order, and equations and tables in a PDF show as math and tables.
- A translation no longer stops halfway when one reply comes back in the wrong format.

## 0.2.22

- ⌘+ and ⌘− (Ctrl on Windows) make the paper's text bigger or smaller, from 70 to 150 %; ⌘0 puts it back.
- Paste a paper link from another site (ACL Anthology, PMLR, NeurIPS, a journal page…) or a DOI into the arXiv box. The arXiv version opens when there is one, otherwise the site's PDF.

## 0.2.21

- Dragging a paper up or down in a folder shows a teal line where it will land.

## 0.2.20

- Translate on a search, AI search or roadmap result now works in a new tab behind the list, with a spinner on the button, so you can start several papers in a row.

## 0.2.19

- A new version now shows its Update button within 15 minutes, or as soon as you come back to the window, without restarting the app.

## 0.2.18

- ⌘T (Ctrl+T on Windows) opens a new tab, so you can keep several papers or searches open. ⌘W closes a tab, ⌃Tab and ⌘1–9 switch tabs, and ⌘-click opens a paper in a new tab.
- Picking Easy starts the easy version right away; there is no second button to press.
- Show only the original or only the translation with the switch at the top of the paper. A running translation shows a spinner and its count.

## 0.2.17

- Drag the top of the chat panel to move it; double-click there to put it back.

## 0.2.16

- Buttons, links and highlights use the same teal as the logo; errors and warnings stay red.

## 0.2.15

- A copy button under each summary and answer copies the whole reply.

## 0.2.14

- Model names show their version, such as Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5.

## 0.2.13

- Select a word or a short passage and click Translate in the bubble to see what it means in that sentence.

## 0.2.12

- A new teal logo that matches the website.

## 0.2.11

- The Find papers and Roadmap tabs stay at the top of AI search while the conversation scrolls.

## 0.2.10

- AI search has two tabs: Find papers, and Roadmap for a reading roadmap from a topic or a paper.
- A roadmap waits and tries again when arXiv is briefly busy.

## 0.2.9

- In AI search, ask for a roadmap on a topic, like "I want to research LLM memory": it picks a starting paper on arXiv and builds the roadmap from it.

## 0.2.8

- Roadmap, next to Summarize: what to read before and after a paper, built from its real references and the papers that cite it. In AI search you can also just ask for a roadmap.
- Hover a paper in the sidebar and click the check to mark it read. Click it again to clear it.
- Backups in Model settings stay folded until you open them.

## 0.2.7

- Buttons, menus and messages follow the language you translate into: Korean, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, French, German or Portuguese.
- The translation column shows the language's own name.

## 0.2.6

- Backups in Model settings: when your model hits a usage limit or can't run, the next backup you picked takes over by itself.
- The sidebar shows which model is answering, and Model settings says why a backup took over. The usual model is tried again within 20 minutes.

## 0.2.5

- Connect through OpenGateway: one API key for Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek and more. It works as soon as the key is saved.
- API key fields link to the page where you create a key.
- Model lists from API services leave out models that can't chat, such as image models.

## 0.2.4

- Model settings show the model in use and each service's logo. Pick the model from a list instead of typing its name.
- Codex lists the models your ChatGPT login can use.
- The Update button asks before it installs, and the app shows what changed after an update.

## 0.2.3

- Run OpenPaper on Codex, the Anthropic API, or any OpenAI-compatible API such as OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Gemini or Ollama, as well as on Claude Code.
- The app runs on Windows, with its own setup program and updates.
- Reopen a paper where you stopped reading.
- Find text in both columns with ⌘F (Ctrl+F on Windows).
